Princess Mariam de Bagration (born 27 June 1947) is a Spanish-Georgian noblewoman (tavadi) of the House of Mukhrani.
Early life

Princess Mariam was born on 27 June 1947 in Madrid. She is a dentist. She is a daughter of Prince Irakli Bagration of Mukhrani and Infanta María de las Mercedes de Baviera y Borbón. Marriage and issue

In 1968 she married Spanish Ambassador José Luis Blanco-Briones y de Cuéllar (1935-1985).
