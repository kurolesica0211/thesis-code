Helen Mary Malcolm (15 March 1918 – 13 October 2010) was one of the first two regular female announcers on BBC Television after the Second World War and was a household name in the United Kingdom during the 1950s.
Her autobiography, Me, was published in 1956. Personal life

Malcolm was first married to Sir Basil Bartlett, a baronet and actor, on 5 January 1937. They had three daughters, Lucy, Jemima, and Annabel, before the marriage ended in divorce in 1960. She married secondly Colin McFadyean, a solicitor, on 16 June 1960, gaining two step-daughters: Andrea, who died in 1983, and Melanie McFadyean, who became a journalist.
