Samuel Chatto (born 28 July 1996) is a British ceramic artist.
He is a member of the extended British royal family as the elder child of Lady Sarah Chatto and Daniel Chatto, both of whom are artists. He is a grandson of Princess Margaret, Countess of Snowdon and a great-nephew of Elizabeth II. As of November 2025, he is 30th in line to the British throne. He also has a younger brother, Arthur, with whom he attended Eton College.
Chatto has spent time in Japan to learn pottery techniques. His work has been displayed in various places, including a restaurant in Somerset and art galleries in Norway and Japan. In 2019, Chatto spent six weeks in India to complete 200 hours of training to become a yoga instructor. Chatto does not have any official royal duties, but has attended several events such as the state funeral of Elizabeth II, the coronation of Charles III and Camilla, and the wedding of Prince Harry and Meghan Markle.
