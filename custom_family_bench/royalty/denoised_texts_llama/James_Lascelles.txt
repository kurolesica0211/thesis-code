James Edward Lascelles (born 5 October 1953) is an English musician and the second son of the 7th Earl of Harewood and his first wife, Marion.
Lascelles is a second cousin to King Charles III. Music

When young, Lascelles had classical piano and drum lessons, and claims that "John Tavener 'taught' him to improvise" by performing duets on a church organ. He then became interested in jazz, blues, and rock and roll. Global Village Trucking Company

Lascelles was a co-founder of the Global Village Trucking Company, known to its fans as "The Globs", in the early 1970s.
