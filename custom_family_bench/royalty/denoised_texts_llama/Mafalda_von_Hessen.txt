Mafalda Margarethe Prinzessin und Landgräfin von Hessen (born 6 July 1965), known also as Princess Mafalda of Hesse, is a German aristocrat and fashion designer.
In autumn 2015, she released her latest collection. She was named in Vanity Fair's 2007 International Best-Dressed List. Marriages and children

Mafalda first married Count Enrico Marone Cinzano on 8 July 1989, but they divorced soon after. The marriage was childless.
Second, she married Carlo Galdo on 19 December 1991. They have two daughters. The couple divorced in 1999. Third, Mafalda married Count Ferdinando Brachetti Peretti on 14 July 2000.
