Adélaïde de Clermont-Tonnerre (born 20 March 1976) is a French journalist and author.
Biography

Adélaïde de Clermont-Tonnerre was born in Neuilly-sur-Seine, France on 20 March 1976, a descendant of Princess Isabelle d'Orléans. She won five literary prizes for her first novel and was a finalist for the Goncourt prize for début fiction. Her second novel won the Grand Prix du Roman in 2016. Clermont-Tonnerre attended the Ecole Normale Supérieure though she didn't complete.
