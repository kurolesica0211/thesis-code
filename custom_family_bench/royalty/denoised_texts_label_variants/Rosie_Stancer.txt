Rose Cecilia "Rosie" Stancer (née Clayton; born 25 January 1960) is a British explorer and polar adventurer, and a female-line second cousin of King Charles III of the UK. Stancer is the daughter of Lady Mary Cecilia Leveson-Gower (1917–2014) and Sir Samuel Clayton. Her maternal grandparents were Rose Leveson-Gower, Countess Granville (an elder sister of Queen Elizabeth the Queen Mother), and William Leveson-Gower, 4th Earl Granville, naval commander, making her a second cousin of King Charles III. Her paternal grandfather was Brigadier-General Sir Gilbert Falkingham Clayton, British Army intelligence officer and colonial administrator.
