In 1995 Prince Oskar took a Ph.D. Order of Saint John (Bailiwick of Brandenburg)

In 1999 Prince Oskar was appointed Herrenmeister (Grand Master) of the Order of Saint John (Bailiwick of Brandenburg), following his father Prince Wilhelm-Karl of Prussia and his grandfather Prince Oskar of Prussia who both previously served as Herrenmeister. Active in promoting the numerous charitable and volunteer organisations connected with the Order of St John, in 2013 Prince Oskar represented the Bailiwick at an ecumenical celebration in Berlin to observe the Knights Hospitallers' 900th anniversary.
