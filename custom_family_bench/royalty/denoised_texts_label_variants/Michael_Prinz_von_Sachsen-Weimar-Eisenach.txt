Michael Prinz von Sachsen-Weimar-Eisenach (born 15 November 1946) is the current head of the Grand Ducal House of Saxe-Weimar-Eisenach, as well as the most senior agnate of the entire House of Wettin. Early life

Michael was born in Bamberg, Bavaria, the only son of Hereditary Grand Duke Charles Augustus of Saxe-Weimar-Eisenach and Baroness Elisabeth von Wangenheim-Winterstein (1912–2010). Among his godparents were Queen Juliana of the Netherlands and the Grand Duchess Anastasia of Russia imposter, Anna Anderson, who was living with his aunt Princess Luise of Saxe-Meiningen. When his father died on 14 October 1988, Michael succeeded him as Head of the House of Saxe-Weimar-Eisenach. Therefore, the Grand Ducal House of Saxe-Weimar-Eisenach will most likely become extinct in the male line.
