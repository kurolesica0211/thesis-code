Adélaïde de Clermont-Tonnerre (born 20 March 1976) is a French journalist and author. Biography

Adélaïde de Clermont-Tonnerre was born in Neuilly-sur-Seine, France on 20 March 1976, a descendant of Princess Isabelle d'Orléans. Clermont-Tonnerre attended the Ecole Normale Supérieure though she didn't complete. Clermont-Tonnerre then became a columnist and journalist, currently working as the section editor on Point de Vue.
