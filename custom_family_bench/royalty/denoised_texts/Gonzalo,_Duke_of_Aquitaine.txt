Don Gonzalo, Duke of Aquitaine (5 June 1937 – 27 May 2000) (Spanish: Gonzalo Víctor Alfonso José Bonifacio Antonio María y Todos los Santos de Borbón y Dampierre; French: Gonzalve Victor Alphonse Joseph Boniface Antoine Marie Toussaint de Bourbon) was a grandson of Alfonso XIII, King of Spain. Life

Gonzalo was born in the Sant'Anna Clinic in Rome on 5 June 1937, the younger son of Infante Jaime, Duke of Segovia and his first wife, Emmanuelle de Dampierre. Also on 21 September 1972, Jaime gave Gonzalo the title duc d'Aquitaine (Duke of Aquitaine).
