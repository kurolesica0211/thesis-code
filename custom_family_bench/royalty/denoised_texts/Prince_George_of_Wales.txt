Prince George of Wales (George Alexander Louis; born 22 July 2013) is a member of the British royal family. He is the eldest child of William, Prince of Wales, and Catherine, Princess of Wales, and the eldest grandchild of King Charles III and Diana, Princess of Wales. He is the eldest child of Prince William and Catherine (then known as Duke and Duchess of Cambridge). He has a younger sister and brother, Princess Charlotte and Prince Louis. Upbringing

George spent his first months in Anglesey, Wales, before his family relocated to Kensington Palace in 2014. Public image

Clothing and other products used by George are often sold out, a phenomenon termed the "Prince George effect". Title and styles

George is a British prince with the official style and title "His Royal Highness Prince George of Wales". During the reign of his great-grandmother, he was styled "His Royal Highness Prince George of Cambridge".
